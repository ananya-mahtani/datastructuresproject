# RideShare Matching Project for the 5C's

## This project works best on Google Colab, so copy and paste the code text file in Google Colab for best experience


Simply enter your name, phone number, your flight, and the times you'd like to arrive at the airport, and hit that "Add Rider" Button!

To see what car you are assigned in, click on the "Group Riders" button!

If you want to remove yourself from the system, simply enter your phone number into the last box, and hit the "Cancel by Phone" button!

Save money on car rides, enjoy your flight!
